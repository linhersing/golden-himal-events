Video requirements: film yourself singing

Required: one continuous 60–90-second video showing your own face and real singing voice clearly. No audio-only file, photo slideshow, lip-syncing, pitch correction, filters that conceal your face or video cuts. The organizer checks the recording.

How to film clearly: picture, lighting, sound and video link
  • Picture: 1080p (1920 × 1080) preferred; at least 720p (1280 × 720) recommended. Landscape 16:9 is preferred, but a clear vertical phone video is acceptable. These are shooting recommendations, not newly approved V2 entry rules.
  • Framing: keep your face, mouth and upper body visible throughout. Put your phone on a steady surface; do not film only the ceiling, a photo or the floor.
  • Lighting: record in a bright room, preferably facing a window or lamp. Avoid a bright window behind you, shadows covering your face or a dark image.
  • Sound: choose a quiet room, keep the phone close enough, and check that the singing is louder than backing music. Avoid distortion, clipping and wind noise. Listen once before sharing.
  • File: if the video platform asks for a format, MP4 (H.264) at about 30 fps is recommended. This site currently accepts a video LINK, not the original file, so there is no website file-size limit to promise.
  • Sharing: set YouTube to Public or Unlisted, or set Google Drive to “Anyone with the link — Viewer.” Open the link in a private/incognito window: the video should play without asking for an account, approval or access request.
