Important news in short expandable cards. Details below are from the latest V2 planning record and remain subject to organizer confirmation.

PREVIEW · Not an official opening announcement

Audition schedule (V2 plan)

Online audition windows proposed for January 1–31, 2027 and April 1–30, 2027 (Nepal local time). Live qualifiers and finals are planned for later in 2027. Dates and venues require organizer confirmation before the public launch.

Who can enter? (V2 eligibility)

University category: currently enrolled college or university students. Community category: applicants aged 18 or older who are not university students. Eligible provinces in the V2 draft: Bagmati and Gandaki. An applicant may enter only one category per competition round.

Prepare your audition video (60–90 seconds)

Record your own continuous singing performance. Your face and voice should be clearly visible and audible. No lip-syncing, voice correction or stitched clips. For the current TEST portal, share a publicly viewable YouTube or Google Drive link. The video must still be checked by the organizer.

TEST payment and public registration status

Invited accounts can rehearse the application and checkout journey without paying. A TEST checkout receipt means only that the simulation has completed. Public signup, real payment and formal admission are not yet open.
