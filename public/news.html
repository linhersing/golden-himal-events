0202 / ELIGIBILITYWho may enter?University and community categories

Eligible singers from all Nepal provinces may apply. University entrants need proof of current enrollment. Community entrants must be at least 18 on their audition deadline and must not be current university students.

Qualification documents

A valid student ID or school certificate is required for university applicants. Secure document uploading is not yet available in this test; do not share identity documents using public links.

0303 / AUDITION VIDEOPrepare your video60–90 seconds · continuous singing

Record 60–90 seconds of continuous singing with a clear face and voice. No lip-syncing, voice correction or cuts. During TEST, submit a viewable YouTube or Google Drive link; duration is checked by the organizer.

Final songs and accompaniment

Finalists select one song from the organizer’s list and confirm the song and key by the announced deadline.

0404 / REGISTRATION STATUSTEST portal statusPayment and public registration are not open

Approved TEST accounts may rehearse registration and simulated checkout. No money is charged. A TEST completion receipt does not mean officially accepted registration.

Fee and future rounds

The rules specify NPR 500 per audition. The April audition is not open, and advancement requires no additional fee. No real payments are accepted here.

Voting, awards and appeals

Website votes count for 51% and judges for 49%. Raise objections with the organizer within 48 hours of the result announcement.
